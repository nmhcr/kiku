How it works

  1. Create a project: upload a .txt, .md or .epub book and give it a title.
  2. Adjust the voice, pacing, chapter gaps and how the audio is split.
  3. Synthesize chapters. Each chapter becomes one audio clip, and progress is resumable.
  4. Assemble the finished chapters into one file, or into several by chapter count or file count.

tiny-voca is English only, CPU-only by default, and everything runs locally. The voice is fixed (F2 or M2); speed, pitch and gaps shape delivery, not the speaker.
