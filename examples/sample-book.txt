A short sample book for the audiobook creator scaffold. The text is split
into chapters by headings such as "Chapter One". tiny-voca reads English only.

Chapter One: The Beginning

Hello! This is a tiny audiobook made with tiny-voca. Long text is fine: it is
read one sentence at a time and the audio is joined together. Chapter headings
like this one become separate audio files.

Chapter Two: A Little More

Each chapter can be synthesized on its own, so a long book does not have to be
rendered in one go. Progress is saved in manifest.json, which means an
interrupted run can be resumed without starting over. When every chapter is
ready, the assemble command joins them into a single audiobook file.
